# Multimodal Visual Question Answering in Neuro-oncology and Gastroenterology: A Domain-Specific Feasibility Study

## Dataset Repository

This repository is intended to host the datasets introduced in the manuscript:

**"Multimodal Visual Question Answering in Neuro-oncology and Gastroenterology: A Domain-Specific Feasibility Study"**

currently under review at **Computers in Biology and Medicine (Elsevier)**.

The study introduces two novel domain-specific Medical Visual Question Answering (Med-VQA) datasets designed to support research on multimodal artificial intelligence systems combining medical image understanding and natural language processing.

---

## Datasets

### Brain Tumor Med-VQA Dataset

This dataset consists of brain MRI images associated with clinically relevant question-answer pairs. The dataset includes cases belonging to the following tumor categories:

* Glioma
* Meningioma
* Pituitary tumor

### Gastrointestinal Med-VQA Dataset

This dataset consists of gastrointestinal endoscopic images associated with clinically relevant question-answer pairs. The dataset includes images related to:

* Polyps
* Ulcerative colitis
* Esophagitis

The primary objective of both datasets is to support the development and evaluation of multimodal Visual Question Answering systems in specialized medical domains.

## Availability

The manuscript describing these datasets is currently under peer review.

Until the review process is completed, the datasets are **not publicly available through this repository**.

Researchers interested in accessing the datasets for academic and non-commercial research purposes may submit a motivated and reasonable request to the corresponding author.
