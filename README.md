# Visual Question Answering (VQA)

This repository collects research activities, software artifacts, datasets, and experimental results related to **Visual Question Answering (VQA)** and multimodal Artificial Intelligence developed by the **Language and Knowledge Engineering (LKE)** Research Group at **ICAR-CNR**.

The research focuses on AI systems capable of understanding and reasoning over visual and textual information, combining advances in:

* Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
* Multimodal Learning
* Visual Question Answering (VQA)
* Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
* Knowledge-Enhanced and Neurosymbolic AI
* Explainable and Trustworthy AI

The goal is to develop intelligent systems that can interpret images, documents, and complex visual scenes, answer natural language questions, and support decision-making in real-world domains such as healthcare, cultural heritage, public administration, industry, and robotics.

For information about the research group, projects, publications, and collaborations, please visit the official LKE webpage:
